About this draft

This page is a proposed website privacy policy. It describes the intended website configuration and must be reviewed against actual information handling practices before becoming effective. It is not an application-specific privacy policy.

01

Introduction

This draft Privacy Policy relates to the website being prepared at theaecfllc.online under the name THE AECF,LLC.

Its purpose is to explain the information handling practices associated with the website, including information collection, use, disclosure, retention and available privacy choices.

The identity and contact details of the responsible organization will be confirmed before this policy is finalized.

02

Information We May Collect

Information provided voluntarily

Once official communication channels are operational, correspondence may contain information voluntarily provided by visitors, including:

  • Name and email address.
  • The subject and contents of an enquiry.
  • Information necessary to respond to a request.
  • Other details voluntarily included in correspondence.

Technical information

Website infrastructure providers may process technical information necessary to deliver, maintain and protect their services.

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Requested pages and access timestamps.
  • Technical diagnostic and security information.

The current website source code does not include account registration, payment processing, file uploads or an embedded contact submission form.

03

How Information May Be Used

Where relevant and permitted by applicable law, information may be processed for purposes including:

  • Operating and maintaining the website.
  • Responding to enquiries and communications.
  • Providing requested information.
  • Investigating technical or security issues.
  • Preventing unauthorized access and misuse.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

These purposes must be reviewed against actual operational practices before publication of the final policy.

04

Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable laws require a legal basis for personal information processing, that basis depends on the specific activity and its purpose.

Potential legal bases include:

  • Consent: Where valid consent is required and obtained.
  • Contractual necessity: Where processing is needed for a contract or requested pre-contractual steps.
  • Legitimate interests: Where permitted and supported by an appropriate assessment of individual rights and interests.
  • Legal obligations: Where processing is required by applicable law.

Applicable jurisdictions and specific legal bases remain subject to final review.

05

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The current website design does not intentionally install advertising cookies, analytics trackers, tracking pixels or persistent browser storage identifiers.

Hosting and infrastructure providers may separately process information necessary to deliver and secure their services.

If optional cookies, analytics or advertising technologies are introduced, the privacy policy must be updated and appropriate consent mechanisms implemented where required.

Visitors can manage cookie preferences through their browser settings.

06

Information Sharing

Where necessary and legally permitted, information may be disclosed to relevant service providers or other authorized recipients.

Potential recipients include:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Email and communication service providers.
  • Authorized professional advisers.
  • Authorities where disclosure is legally required.

The current website is not designed to facilitate targeted advertising or the sale of visitor data. Actual information sharing arrangements must be confirmed before this policy becomes effective.

07

Third-Party Services

Third-party services may support website hosting, domain registration, DNS management and business communications.

GitHub Pages

GitHub Pages is the intended website hosting platform. Information about GitHub's privacy practices is available in the GitHub General Privacy Statement .

Namecheap

Namecheap is the domain registrar. Visitors may review the Namecheap Privacy Policy for information about its services.

External websites

External websites may apply their own privacy policies. Visitors should review those policies before providing personal information.

08

Data Security

Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards should be implemented according to the nature of information processed and associated risks.

Planned website safeguards include:

  • Encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Protected administrative accounts.
  • Appropriate access controls.
  • Responsible handling of communications.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

Visitors should avoid sending passwords, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary email.

09

Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only as necessary for its relevant purpose, taking into account applicable legal obligations and legitimate operational requirements.

Retention considerations may include:

  • The time required to respond to correspondence.
  • Resolution of outstanding requests.
  • Applicable legal record-keeping requirements.
  • Security and dispute resolution needs.

Specific retention periods, deletion procedures and provider arrangements must be confirmed during final review.

10

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and individual circumstances, visitors may have rights concerning their personal information.

These rights may include:

  • Access to personal information.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Objection to processing where applicable.
  • Data portability where applicable.
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Complaints to an appropriate data protection authority.

Applicable request procedures, contact details and response requirements will be confirmed before the policy becomes effective.

11

Children's Privacy

The current website is designed to provide general company information. It does not include features specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Applicable children's privacy requirements must be considered if interactive features or products intended for children are introduced.

Individual applications may have different audience classifications and privacy requirements.

12

International Data Transfers

Website and communications infrastructure may operate across different countries and regions.

Where information is transferred internationally, applicable data protection requirements and appropriate transfer safeguards must be considered.

Relevant provider locations and transfer arrangements remain subject to confirmation.

13

Mobile Applications and Digital Products

This draft relates to the corporate website only. It does not establish the privacy practices of any mobile application or digital product.

Application-specific privacy policies must accurately reflect the actual functionality and information handling practices of each application.

Relevant disclosures may include:

  • Personal information and device data collected.
  • Application permissions and their purposes.
  • Advertising and analytics technologies.
  • Third-party software development kits.
  • Account management and deletion procedures.
  • Data sharing, security and retention.
  • Audience and children's privacy requirements.

No particular application, software service or product is represented as currently available through this draft policy.

14

Changes to This Privacy Policy

The finalized Privacy Policy should be maintained to reflect changes in website functionality, information handling practices, service providers and applicable requirements.

An effective date will be added after review and approval of the actual privacy practices.

Material changes should be communicated through appropriate mechanisms where required.

15

Contact Information

Verified business contact information will be published here before this policy is finalized.
