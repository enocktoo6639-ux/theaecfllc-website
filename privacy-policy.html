Scope of this Privacy Policy

This notice covers our corporate website and related communications. Individual mobile applications may involve different information practices, which must be explained in their respective privacy policies.

01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes information handling associated with the corporate website of THE AECF,LLC, accessible at theaecfllc.online .

THE AECF,LLC is a mobile application development company with a broad focus on software, digital products and mobile experiences across diverse application categories.

We recognize the importance of privacy, transparency and responsible information handling in the development and operation of digital products.

This policy explains website-related data, its potential uses, service providers, privacy choices and how to contact us regarding privacy matters.

02

Information We Collect

Information provided voluntarily

When you contact us by email, information provided through your communication may include:

  • Your name and email address.
  • The subject and contents of your message.
  • Application names and relevant technical details.
  • Information necessary to investigate a support request.
  • Other information you voluntarily provide.

Please provide only the information necessary for your enquiry. Do not send passwords, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Technical website information

Our corporate website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed into a GitHub account.

Infrastructure providers may also process technical information required to deliver and protect their services, such as:

  • Browser and device information.
  • Requested website resources.
  • Access dates and times.
  • Diagnostic and security information.

The current corporate website does not include an account registration system, payment processing system, embedded contact submission form or file upload functionality.

03

How We Use Information

Information received through website-related communications may be used for purposes including:

  • Responding to business enquiries.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Investigating application-related questions.
  • Considering product feedback and suggestions.
  • Maintaining website functionality.
  • Investigating security and technical concerns.
  • Responding to privacy and data-related requests.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

The specific purpose of processing depends on the nature of the information and the circumstances in which it was provided.

04

Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable data protection laws require a legal basis for processing personal information, the relevant basis depends on the activity and its purpose.

  • Consent: When consent is required and validly obtained.
  • Contractual necessity: Where processing is needed to perform a contract or respond to requested pre-contractual steps.
  • Legitimate interests: Where permitted and appropriately balanced against individual privacy rights.
  • Legal obligations: Where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law.

These bases apply only where relevant under the laws governing the particular processing.

05

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The current corporate website source code does not intentionally implement advertising cookies, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or persistent browser-storage identifiers.

Website hosting and infrastructure providers may independently process technical information necessary to deliver and protect their services.

Individual mobile applications may use different technologies, including analytics or advertising services, where applicable. Such practices must be disclosed in the relevant application policy.

Visitors can manage browser cookies and related preferences through their browser settings.

06

Information Sharing

Website-related information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, security and email communications.

Information may also be disclosed where necessary and permitted by law, including to:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Email and communications service providers.
  • Authorized professional advisers, where necessary.
  • Authorities where disclosure is legally required.

The corporate website does not contain advertising technology designed to sell or exchange visitor information.

Any data sharing associated with individual mobile applications must be addressed separately in their applicable privacy disclosures.

07

Third-Party Services

GitHub Pages

GitHub Pages provides hosting infrastructure for this corporate website.

Information about GitHub's handling of personal information is available in the GitHub General Privacy Statement .

Namecheap

Namecheap provides domain registration and DNS-related services for this website.

Private Email

Namecheap Private Email provides the email infrastructure used for business communications through contact@theaecfllc.online.

Information about Namecheap's privacy practices is available in the Namecheap Privacy Policy .

External websites and application services

External services may have independent privacy policies and terms. Third-party services integrated into individual applications must be identified in their applicable disclosures.

08

Data Security

Our corporate website uses HTTPS to encrypt connections between visitors and the website's hosting infrastructure.

Appropriate safeguards for personal information depend on the nature of the information, the relevant systems and associated risks.

Administrative access, business correspondence and application-related information require suitable security measures and responsible handling.

No method of electronic communication, transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

If you identify a potential security or privacy concern, please contact contact@theaecfllc.online .

09

Data Retention

Retention of website-related communications depends on the purpose of the correspondence, operational requirements and applicable legal obligations.

Relevant retention considerations include:

  • Responding to enquiries and support requests.
  • Resolving outstanding issues.
  • Maintaining necessary business records.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.
  • Protecting legitimate rights and resolving disputes.

Hosting and email service providers maintain information according to their applicable service arrangements and retention practices.

For details regarding the retention of your correspondence or to request deletion, contact us using the email address provided in this policy.

Application-specific retention periods and deletion procedures must be disclosed separately for each relevant application.

10

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable laws and your circumstances, you may have rights regarding your personal information.

  • Access to personal information.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Objection to processing where applicable.
  • Data portability where applicable.
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Complaints to a competent data protection authority.

You may submit a privacy enquiry through contact@theaecfllc.online .

Additional information may be necessary to verify your identity and understand the scope of your request.

11

Data Deletion Requests

If you wish to request deletion of personal information associated with your communications with THE AECF,LLC, please contact our business email.

Submitting a request

  • Send an email to contact@theaecfllc.online .
  • Use the subject line "Privacy and Data Deletion Request".
  • Identify the relevant correspondence or application, if applicable.
  • Explain what information you wish to have deleted.

The ability to delete information depends on the relevant data, applicable legal obligations and any legitimate grounds for continued retention.

This corporate website does not provide website user accounts.

For mobile applications that support user accounts, application-specific account deletion mechanisms and instructions must be provided in accordance with applicable requirements. This general contact channel does not replace those mechanisms.

12

Children's Privacy

Our corporate website provides general company information and does not include interactive features specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Mobile applications may have different intended audiences and age classifications. Applications intended for children or mixed audiences require appropriate age-related privacy safeguards and disclosures.

Parents or guardians who have concerns about personal information involving a child may contact us using the email address in this policy.

13

International Data Transfers

Website hosting, email and other digital infrastructure may operate in different countries or regions.

Information processed through these services may therefore be handled outside the country where a visitor resides.

International transfers are subject to relevant legal requirements and applicable service provider arrangements.

You may contact us for further information about transfers relevant to your personal information.

14

Mobile Applications and Digital Products

Mobile Apps Games Entertainment Education Productivity Business Sports Lifestyle Utilities Multimedia Digital Innovation

Individual applications may differ in their functionality, technical infrastructure, permissions, intended audience and information handling practices.

Application-specific privacy disclosures

Each application requires privacy information appropriate to its actual functionality and data practices, including, where applicable:

  • The purposes for which information is processed.
  • Application permissions and their purposes.
